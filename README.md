## 🌱 About Mati Carbon

**Mati** is a climate-tech company scaling **enhanced rock weathering (ERW)** — spreading crushed basalt rock dust on smallholder farms to:
- Remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere (durable, verifiable carbon removal)
- Correct soil acidity and release nutrients
- Raise crop yields and farmer incomes
- Build climate resilience for smallholder farmers

**Mission:** Remove 100 million tonnes of CO₂ while improving the lives of smallholder farmers across the Global South.

**Where they operate:** Currently active across seven countries, with major projects in India (Chhattisgarh, Seoni) — spanning Asia and Africa.

**Founder:** Shantanu Agarwal (serial climate-tech entrepreneur)

**Notable milestones:**
- Certified carbon removal credits under Isometric's ERW protocol (Seoni & North Chhattisgarh projects)
- Verified removal credits under Puro Standard's ERW methodology
- Grantee of the Bezos Earth Fund Acceleration Initiative (with The Earthshot Prize)
- Five-year research agreement with the Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
